## Design (in the code)
- Link-map nodes live in a static of ld.so (`dlopen::NODES`); start fills the public prefix (`dlopen::start_node`,
  `link_next`) in `ld.rs::rendezvous_start` before relocation; the vDSO's link map is a separate static
  (`debug::VDSO_MAP`, `vdso_node`) linked after the program; `View::next` steps over it (`debug::past_vdso`).
- `debug::start` fills `_r_debug`, DT_DEBUG (via `niji_ld::reloc::dynamic_word`), RT_ADD + r_brk;
  `debug::keep_copy` right after `protect_relro` (program headers still mapped: reading them after the views'
  munmap was the SIGSEGV of leg 1), `debug::consistent` before `run_init`. dlopen: RT_ADD before linking,
  RT_CONSISTENT after publish; dlclose: RT_DELETE before the first unload, RT_CONSISTENT after.
- Exports `_r_debug` / `_dl_debug_state` are defined in `ld.rs` (unsafe-audit permits `export` there only).
