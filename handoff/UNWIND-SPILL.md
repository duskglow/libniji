## What changed

- `crates/rt/src/unwind.rs`: the `Memory` trait gains `spill_map` / `spill_bytes` / `spill_unmap` (default:
  no pages). `OwnMemory` maps anonymous private pages through `Sys::mmap`. `Spill` holds fixed-size records past
  an inline area: one page first, then a mapping twice the size with a copy when full, unmapped on drop.
  `run` keeps 4 remembered rows inline and spills deeper ones (a row is encoded as 18 cells of 16 bytes); `Stack` keeps
  32 entries inline and spills deeper ones. `row_at` and `run` take the memory. The spill path is `#[cold]`.
- Why mmap and not the heap: the walk runs from `backtrace` in signal handlers and from the thread exit's
  handler walk (D-22.23). An mmap is async-signal-safe and takes no lock, so the spill needs no context test.
  A mapping that cannot be had fails only that row or expression, which ends the walk at that frame as the caps did.
- Premise correction: libniji exports no `_Unwind_*` (D-2.11). `_Unwind_Backtrace` and C++ throws use libgcc's
  unwinder, which is not libniji code. This unwinder serves `backtrace`, the `mtrace` caller and `handler_above`
  (D-22.23). The tests therefore drive `backtrace` (conformance) and the walker's layers (unit tests).

## Tests

- rt unit tests: `remember_state_spills_past_the_inline_rows` (5, 10 and 1000 levels, every restore count, every
  rule kind through a spilled row), `expression_stack_spills_past_the_inline_entries` (32+1, 100 and 500 deep; pick
  into the spill, the inline part and the bottom; the budget still ends 1025 pushes),
  `a_spill_that_cannot_be_mapped_fails_the_row` (Faulty mmap ENOMEM on the first mapping and on a regrowth; nothing
  left mapped). The old `remember_depth_is_bounded` and the full-stack case still hold with `NoMemory` (no pages).
- conformance `s02_backtrace`, clause s2_7_c_1: `deep_remember` (hand-written CFI with 10 nested
  `.cfi_remember_state`, each level with its own CFA offset, all restored before the call) and `deep_expr` (CFA from
  a 100-entry expression) are traced through to their callers.
- Plants: `handoff/UNWIND-SPILL-plants.txt` (each cap restored alone, unit and end to end; regrowth copy; unmap;
  cell encoding).
